Why Revstage

Clear BOM control, without taking on a full PLM system.

Small electronics teams need to know which revision is released, whether each reference is accounted for, which alternates are approved, and where parts are stocked. Revstage focuses on those everyday decisions instead of supplier networks or production planning.

What it does

Designed around the questions your team asks every day.

These are the core capabilities Revstage is being developed to bring together.

01

BOM revisions

Keep released revisions easy to find and review proposed changes in context.

02

Reference designators

Check that reference designators and line quantities agree, and spot missing or duplicate entries.

03

Approved alternates

Track primary manufacturer parts and alternatives with clear approved, conditional, or blocked status.

04

Where-used context

Trace the products and revisions that use a part before making a change.

05

Inventory locations

Connect part records to on-hand quantities and the bins where stock is kept.

06

Local-first workspace

Keep BOM work under your control without depending on a hosted BOM cloud or a server to run.

Revstage is not an ERP, purchasing system, supplier quote service, or compliance product.

Data ownership

Your BOM workspace stays under your control.

Revstage is designed around local workspaces. Core BOM work does not depend on storing your design data in a hosted cloud service. Read the privacy policy and support page for details.

No claim of certification or compliance is made.
